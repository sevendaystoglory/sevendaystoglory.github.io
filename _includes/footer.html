I love image/video to ASCII components. It is surprisingly hard to make an ASCII picture that is soothing to look at. I had a brilliant reference with a post by Lucas Jin showing a video-to-ASCII renderer that actually looks good. For the curious, in short -- the picture is divided into a grid of cells, where each cell's brightness picks a character from a ramp that runs from light (.) to dense (@). What really sells it is that cells that sit on an edge get a stroke that follows the edge (| / - \) instead, and each character takes the colour of the pixels underneath. Everything else that moves on this page is basically the same idea. Show your Claude the post and the summary above and it should be able to make beautiful ASCII components. Cheers!
